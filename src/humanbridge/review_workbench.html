Review scenario and label guide

Dimension ratings: Not supported = no sufficient support in the cited evidence. Supporting = a relevant supporting requirement. Central = central to executing the task. Insufficient evidence = context does not support a judgment.

Exposure: Assistive candidate = a bounded supporting substep. Substantial candidate = assistance with a core operation, conditional on documented quality and oversight. These are qualitative hypotheses, not time-saving measurements.

Workflow: An automation candidate needs evidence for the complete bounded operation, inputs, quality checks and oversight. A wording match is never enough. Use insufficient evidence when context is missing.

Keep cultural and creative judgments evidence-based. A job title is not evidence of a person's abilities or worth.
